## Practices and methodologies

- Michael Nygard, [Documenting Architecture Decisions](https://www.cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions), 15 November 2011: a short decision record with context, the decision itself and consequences; a superseded record is kept
- [Data on the Web Best Practices (W3C)](https://www.w3.org/TR/dwbp/), 31 January 2017: update frequency, explaining unavailable data, collecting and publishing feedback
- GSA, [DCAT-US v3.0 Implementation Guide](https://resources.data.gov/resources/dcat-us-3-implementation/), revision 1.1 of 9 September 2026: inventory, a mandatory contact, withdrawing a dataset with an explanation
- [data.gouv.fr guide: publishing a dataset](https://guides.data.gouv.fr/jeux-de-donnees/publier-un-jeu-de-donnees) (in French): with regular updates the record is sent via the API
- [FAIR Principles](https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/)
- [International Open Data Charter](https://opendatacharter.org/principles/), 2015
- [5-star open data](https://5stardata.info/)
- T. E. Hardwicke et al., [Analytic reproducibility in articles receiving open data badges at the journal Psychological Science](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.201494), Royal Society Open Science, 2021: of 25 articles with an open data badge, the checked figures matched in 9 without help from the authors
- [Joint Declaration of Data Citation Principles, FORCE11, 2014](https://force11.org/info/joint-declaration-of-data-citation-principles-final/)
- Research Data Alliance, [Precise, Actionable Reference to Dynamic Data](https://doi.org/10.15497/RDA00132), 2025: how to cite dynamic data and parts of it
- Make Data Count, [Data Citation Corpus](https://makedatacount.org/data-citation/data-citation-corpus-documentation/): a corpus of data citations; [GBIF citation guidelines](https://www.gbif.org/citation-guidelines): download DOIs and derived datasets
- [Creative Commons](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/)
- [Open Definition 2.1](https://opendefinition.org/od/2.1/en/): what counts as an open licence; commercial use allowed, no ban on commercial use or on changes
- [SPDX licence list](https://spdx.org/licenses/)
- [CoreTrustSeal](https://www.coretrustseal.org/)
- [Open Data Maturity](https://data.europa.eu/en/open-data-maturity/2025), [2025 report](https://data.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-12/2025_odm_report_5.pdf): a survey of 36 countries; EU-27 average 86%, all participants 81%; [methodology](https://data.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-12/2025_odm_methodology.pdf): four dimensions at 25% each
- [MQA](https://data.europa.eu/mqa/): an assessment of the records data.europa.eu has harvested from portals; catalog scale 0 to 7.5; [methodology](https://data.europa.eu/mqa/methodology), page of 6 October 2026
- OECD, [Digital Government Index and OURdata Index: 2025 Results and Key Findings](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/digital-government-index-and-open-useful-and-re-usable-data-index_6347ec74-en.html), 16 February 2026: OECD average 0.53 against 0.48 in the [2023 edition](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2023-oecd-open-useful-and-re-usable-data-ourdata-index_a37f51c3-en.html)
- [Global Data Barometer](https://globaldatabarometer.org/), [second edition](https://globaldatabarometer.org/2025/05/the-global-data-barometer-2nd-edition-a-shared-compass-for-navigating-the-data-landscape/), 29 May 2025: 43 countries in Africa and Latin America
- [Open Data Inventory (ODIN)](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/), [2024/25 edition](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/biennialReport2024): 197 countries
- [Open Data Barometer](https://opendatabarometer.org/barometer/): last full edition in May 2017; [Global Open Data Index](https://index.okfn.org/): archived, last edition 2016/17
